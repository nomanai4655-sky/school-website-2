How to Apply

We welcome new students every academic year. Follow the steps below to apply:

  1. Fill out the admission form below (or visit the school office).
  2. Submit required documents (birth certificate, previous school records).
  3. Attend the entrance assessment / interaction.
  4. Receive the admission confirmation letter.
  5. Complete fee payment to secure the seat.

Documents Required

  • Birth Certificate
  • Previous School Transfer Certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport-size Photographs
  • Address Proof
