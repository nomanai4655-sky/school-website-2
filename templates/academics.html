Our Stages of Education

We offer education from Class 1 up to Matric (Class 10), covering the following stages:

Primary Section

Class 1-5: Focus on foundational skills in Math, Science, and English.

Middle Section

Class 6-8: Introduction to Computer Science, Social Studies, and Languages.

Matric Section

Class 9-10: Science group, leading up to the Matriculation (SSC) examination.

Subjects Offered

  • Mathematics
  • Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Computer Science
  • English & Languages
  • Social Studies / Islamiat
  • Physical Education

Our Teaching Approach

Concept Building

Lessons are taught so students understand the "why", not just the "what".

Regular Practice

Consistent classwork and assessments help reinforce every concept taught.

Personal Attention

Teachers stay approachable so students can ask questions and clear doubts.
